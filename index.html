Core Strategy

PTE uses integrated (cross) scoring — one spoken or typed answer earns marks in two skills at once. Your two highest targets (Speaking 88 and Writing 85) are driven by tasks that also feed Reading and Listening. Over-invest in a handful of cross-scored "super-tasks" and the lower targets rise almost for free.

Task Templates

Essay — Universal Template (~20 min)

1 · Introduction
The increasing influence of <topic> in our daily lives has led to various discussions and debates. This issue is crucial because of its influence on <impact area>, affecting both individuals and society. In this essay, I will examine <topic> by considering (advantages and disadvantages / causes and effects), and provide my perspective on the matter.
2 · Body Paragraph 1
One of the main (advantages / causes) related to <topic> is <point 1>. Studies indicate that <topic> (promotes / leads to) <outcome>. Moreover, another significant (advantage / cause) is <point 2>, which strongly affects people's lives.
3 · Body Paragraph 2
On the other hand, there are also (disadvantages / consequences) associated with <topic>. A major (drawback / effect) is <point 3>, which becomes more obvious in situations such as <example>. Furthermore, <point 4> (creates obstacles / causes further impact), influencing both individuals and society.
4 · Conclusion
In conclusion, <topic> has (both advantages and disadvantages / significant causes and effects) that must be considered carefully. Looking ahead, it is essential to focus on maximizing the benefits while reducing the drawbacks.

Summarize Written Text (SWT)

One sentence only — never put a full stop in the middle.
(Noun 1) can be a major aspect that relates to modern society these days, so people must pay close attention to it; therefore, the importance of (Noun 2) must not be overlooked because (Noun 3) may affect (Noun 4), and the text explains that (Noun 5) and (Noun 6) are very important.

Summarize Spoken Text (SST)

The lecture was mainly about [Main Topic]. The speaker first discussed [Noun 1] and [Noun 2], emphasizing their importance. In addition, [Noun 3] and [Noun 4] were highlighted as significant aspects. Moreover, the lecture mentioned [Noun 5], along with [Noun 6] and [Noun 7]. Finally, it was concluded that [Noun 8] and [Noun 9] will have a positive influence.

Describe Image (DI)

1. The given image gives important information about (topic).
2. It points out key details and shows how different aspects are connected.
3. The highest value is (X) and the lowest value is (Y).
4. There are different colours, such as (—, — and —).
5. In conclusion, this image was very informative.

Re-tell Lecture (RL)

1. At the beginning of this lecture, the speaker was talking about (_, _, and _).
2. After that, the speaker mentioned that (_ and _) are very important.
3. Then, the speaker gave an example of (_ and _).
4. From this lecture, I can hear (_ and _).
5. Overall, the lecture was very informative.
